# Svastia

> Svastia (Svastia Genetics Inc.) is a precision-genomics and oncology data company that bundles clinical research products with bioinformatics to accelerate precision cancer therapy. It provides DNA/RNA sequencing, exome and gene-panel assays, immune (B/T-cell) repertoire sequencing, biomarker analytics (Tumor Mutational Burden, Microsatellite Instability), and clinical reporting for hospitals, diagnostics labs, researchers, and CROs. Delivery is through a login-gated web portal; there is no public developer API.
